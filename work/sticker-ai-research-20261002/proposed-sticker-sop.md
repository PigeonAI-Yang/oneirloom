# Design and deliver conversational sticker packs

Status: proposed guidance for review, dated 2026-10-02. This document is not an installed or active SKILL. It does not authorize generation, training, installation, publication, or changes to existing skills. No output has been tested under this proposal.

Use the [research report](report.md) for the rationale and source bibliography. Consult the [design evidence](design-communication-evidence.md), [production evidence](ai-production-evidence.md), and [platform evidence](platform-evidence.md) for claim limits. Follow the existing Understand, Observe, and Design order. Choose production after the design explains the intended response.

## 1. Resolve the conversation and deliverable

Identify the audience, relationship, preceding message, sender's purpose, tone, and expected next response. Distinguish acknowledging receipt from agreement, gratitude from an offer, and playful teasing from hostility. An emotion label alone does not resolve those differences.

Record the requested output: prompts, one sticker, a concept board, individual pack files, or an animated pack. Preserve supplied captions, language, count, and medium. Identify the platform and distribution route when they affect delivery. If the platform is unknown, continue design without inventing a universal export preset.

Produce a short brief in the existing working notes. Ask for missing information only when available evidence cannot settle a choice that blocks the requested outcome. Do not create another ledger or require approval after each stage.

Review the brief through a plausible chat turn. If no occasion explains why someone would send the item, revise its purpose before choosing a pose.

## 2. Understand identity and permitted deformation

Inspect the actual identity reference before the style recipe. Separate visible facts, user-defined meaning, tentative interpretation, and unknown structure. Record the parts, their connections, material, defining color relationships, and meaningful objects.

Separate fixed identity from variable expression. Fixed facts can include appendage count, attachment, an emblem, and the role of a net. Variable expression can include body tilt, compression, reach, orientation, framing, and permitted object interaction. Resolve deformation from the character, rather than requiring an unchanged silhouette.

Reuse an existing usable master reference. Create a character sheet only when unresolved identity construction requires it. Do not invent hidden anatomy to complete a worksheet. Choose a view supported by known structure.

For the supplied DreamWeaver, keep the faceless front and star distinct from a human face. Account for all four tentacles, including intentional occlusion, and preserve their meaningful relationship to the net. Confirm exact construction against the real reference. Do not add eyes, a mouth, human fingers, or new joints to satisfy a reaction recipe.

Produce a compact identity account with allowed changes and unresolved facts. Return to this account whenever a pose changes what a part is or does.

## 3. Observe actual style examples

Assign each reference a role: identity, pose, style, layout, or an output to critique. A reference can have more than one role when that choice is deliberate. Do not assume that a style image locks identity.

Inspect the actual image at its available resolution and intended display size. Describe the first readable cue, shape simplification, gesture, overlap, crop, lettering, material, color, and edge treatment. Explain how those relations direct attention. A phrase such as "cute 3D" does not describe those decisions.

Consult a stored recipe after observing its image. If no image exists, label the recipe as an untested proposal. For original work without style references, state the chosen visual direction without inventing source observations.

Produce a short account of the relations to retain and adapt. Keep uncertain alpha, hidden construction, and fine texture unknown when the source cannot establish them.

## 4. Design the pack and each reaction

Build a compact intent matrix in the existing notes. Compare neighboring responses and remove accidental duplicates. Several items may share a broad meaning when tone, intensity, relationship, or expected reply differs deliberately.

Resolve a reaction card for each item. Use this information without imposing a new file schema:

- A preceding message, audience relationship, sender purpose, and intended next response.
- The exact caption, or an explicit choice to omit text.
- Fixed identity anchors and permitted deformation.
- Body action, appendage relationships, and meaningful prop contact.
- View, crop, overlap order, and the cue that must survive reduction.
- Caption placement and its relationship to the gesture.
- Likely misreadings and a nearby intent that the item must distinguish.

Set shared material, palette relationships, lettering, edge treatment, and visual scale. Vary the action and framing where the response needs it. Avoid identical poses with interchangeable labels.

Review each card as a reply to its preceding message. If an object is present but its contact does not explain the action, repair the design. Decoration and longer prompts cannot resolve missing meaning.

## 5. Calibrate unresolved choices

Select a few varied cases only when they resolve a real uncertainty. A quiet acknowledgment, a strong body action, or an occluded pose may expose different problems. Choose the count from the uncertainty and task scope. There is no standard sample count or mandatory user approval checkpoint.

Inspect actual outputs before expanding an uncertain direction. Compare identity, response meaning, pose clarity, and any critical caption. Keep design uncertainty separate from an entry's failure to execute a resolved instruction.

Produce accepted working choices and observed failures in the same notes. User acceptance remains distinct from internal review. Missing human feedback does not block planning, image inspection, or file checks that can proceed.

## 6. Produce through confirmed capabilities

Check the selected entry's actual inputs, editing controls, output formats, and reference behavior. Vendor documentation establishes documented capability, not local access or successful execution. Preserve the requested model and medium. Keep API controls outside copyable prompt text.

Choose the smallest suitable route:

- Use independent reference-guided generation or edits when a usable master exists. Start each reaction from the master or an appropriate accepted view. Use local iterative edits within a reaction when they preserve successful work.
- Use direct generation when it fits the identity requirement and available evidence. Inspect identity across outputs.
- Use a contact sheet for concept comparison, or for export only when its actual cells can yield complete, adequate individual files. Inspect every crop.
- Use optional lettering, compositing, or alpha repair after an observed defect or an explicit editable-output requirement. Do not require layers, masks, vector code, or external typography by default.
- Consider optional subject tuning only after recurring identity failures in representative reference trials, with a compatible backend and suitable source material. Evaluate unused reactions for both identity and expressive range.

Distinguish multiple input references, several candidates for one job, separate jobs for different reactions, and several cells in one grid. Support for one does not prove support or success for the others. Attribute references by role and request each intended response explicitly.

For prompt-only delivery, resolve complete prompts under the existing language contract. Preserve the same exact caption literal across prompt languages. For production, retain known prompts, inputs, settings, and output links. Do not invent missing metadata.

## 7. Review meaning, identity, and pack behavior

Inspect each result at chat size and picker-thumbnail size. Check action, connections, occlusion, prop contact, crop, and exact caption character by character. OCR and similarity scores may assist inspection but do not establish acceptance.

Inspect actual alpha and composite the output on relevant light, dark, colored, or patterned backgrounds. Check thin appendages, fur, detached marks, text edges, and halos. A painted checkerboard or PNG extension does not prove transparency. A valid alpha channel does not prove correct segmentation.

Compare the pack for identity continuity, shared visual treatment, useful action variety, duplicated responses, and missing functions. Return to individual files after the overview.

When target recipients are available, ask for an unprompted interpretation before revealing the label. Then show a preceding message and ask what the sender means and what response follows. Compare close alternatives such as "received" and "agreed." Record observed interpretations, version, audience, and unresolved ambiguity. AI review can flag concerns but cannot claim human comprehension.

Correct the responsible stage. Revisit identity for changed anatomy, observation for a misread reference, design for ambiguous action, and execution for a clear instruction rendered incorrectly. Preserve successful content. If a critical miss persists through controlled corrections, use the existing bounded diagnosis method. Stop repeating synonymous prompts; return the evidence and unresolved decision. Do not impose new numeric pass scores or restart the whole pack without cause.

For requested animation, design meaningful key poses, action transitions, readable holds, and the loop. Inspect temporal identity, continuity, alpha, first-frame readability, and actual playback. Apply the selected platform's frame, duration, encoding, and size requirements. No universal frame count follows from a research experiment.

## 8. Export and report the exact outcome

Export individual files only after the artwork is usable. Inspect each exported file for dimensions, format, complete edges, caption, alpha where required, and actual animation behavior. Apply requirements for the exact platform asset type and route. Keep recommendations separate from hard limits. Leave unavailable official specifications as source gaps rather than importing numbers from another platform or an unverified mirror.

Deliver the individual files, a pack preview, and a mapping of filenames to intent and exact caption. Include editable source when requested or required by the chosen route. Preserve source references and known production metadata.

Report four separate acceptance results: semantic use, visual family, file validity, and platform behavior. State the evidence and remaining gap for each. A concept board is a preview. Alpha inspection is a file finding. Real chat interpretation needs observed feedback. Platform acceptance requires the corresponding import, display, or review event.

Continue authorized local corrections without repeated permission requests. Publication, uploads, and other external actions retain the current task's authorization boundary. Return a partial result when only a dependent outcome is blocked, with the completed files and the specific missing evidence.
