## Composition and crops

At low detail: warm paper fills the page; an inset cobalt strip occupies the right; the dominant image cluster sits high and right, cropped at top and narrowing toward a lower-right body crop that exits the bottom. Its upper-left pieces can extend above the title, while the lower contour retreats right of the open left field. The title begins in the upper-middle left. The footer sits on the same left axis around y 0.86. Paper between and below these text groups stays quiet.

The splice follows x approximately 0.57 through the occupied image; it is not a drawn divider across empty paper. The left-facing view occupies the left side of this upper-right cluster, with its outward foreshortened star panel visibly above or just right of the title. The right-facing view occupies the right side over cobalt, with its front panel turning toward the right edge and its rounded rear volume toward the splice. This is an opposed-view juxtaposition; continuity is required within each right-view photograph and each left fragment, not between the opposed views.

Back-to-front order: warm paper; cobalt strip; joined image views and local left-view fragments; black typography on exposed paper. Place the title without erasing the left star/panel direction clues. Keep image fragments out of the far-left paper. Keep the right paper border clear.

Crop away cords, slings, the crescent and full limbs. Local tuft, short antenna segments or a small curl may survive only inside the upper-right mass. No antenna arch enters the left void. Selective local cropping and desaturation are authorized identity adaptations; do not invent human anatomy or a long neck to imitate the sculpture.
