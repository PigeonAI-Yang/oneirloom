## What the submitted prompt permits

The prompt asks for an image that "fills almost the entire page height" but does not specify its occupied contour, rightward weight, or lower taper. Explicit thick oval bodies, large antenna forms, and "blue braided cords that cradle a golden crescent" promote new focal shapes in source paper areas. Those instructions explain a design mismatch without establishing a generator defect.

The phrase "compact middle-left headline" has no source-supported start height, area, or size relationship. The prompt explicitly requests two lines. It therefore permits the lower, narrower title and changes the four-line source block without resolving the adaptation. Its "continuous cobalt-blue field fills the right" omits the outer paper border and permits a wide bleed. Staggered horizontal image bands have no bound tying their footprint to the local sculpture contour, so bands across the left field can satisfy that wording.

The title's overlap with fur is a separate output deviation from the prompt's requirement for exposed paper. That real local defect does not account for the major source-to-prompt omissions. The prompt's exact text is rendered, but correct wording and local mechanisms do not validate source fidelity. The available evidence does not establish the generator's internal construction or capability limit.
