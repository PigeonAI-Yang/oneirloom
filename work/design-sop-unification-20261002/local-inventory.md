## Overlap and specific seams found

- **Anchor count versus complete preservation:** the main workflow says to lead with 3–5 checkable anchors (`oneirloom/SKILL.md:14`); reconstruction analysis ranks defining anchors but retains all required relations and mandatory structural anchors (`reconstruction-workflow.md:47-59`). The portrait profile also prioritizes a source-specific focus while explicitly retaining the structural anchors (`profile-portrait.md:7-15`). These statements coexist if “3–5” is the priority set, but the priority-versus-completeness distinction is repeated across layers.
- **Product-first routing is repeated consistently:** the main workflow and routing table, style-design description/trigger, and product-art-direction procedure all place product inspection and concept selection before style/template choice (`oneirloom/SKILL.md:14,18-20`; `oneirloom-style-design/SKILL.md:3,12-13`; `oneirloom-product-art-direction/SKILL.md:12,14-25`).
- **Reference role and identity preservation are distributed:** main routing passes reference roles; visual analysis classifies identity/pose/color/style and separates unknowns; design method separates content input from treatment; poster collection keeps content image separate from its single selected style crop; product art direction preserves product/package identity. Evidence: `oneirloom/SKILL.md:12-14`; `oneirloom-visual-analysis/SKILL.md:21,52`; `oneirloom-style-design/SKILL.md:24`; poster collection `:28-30`; product method `:16-17,25`.
- **Copy/hierarchy appears at several levels:** style-design checklist and style table specify exact text, placement, size, hierarchy, and verification (`oneirloom-style-design/SKILL.md:17-29`; `references/styles.md:5-17`); the poster collection adds exact requested copy, omission of unprovided supporting text, and separate typesetting when the entry cannot render critical copy (`experimental-editorial-posters/template.md:30-34`); local recipes repeat exact sample copy in prompts and acceptance checks.
- **Potential sample-to-acceptance seam (inference):** the collection explicitly says its prefilled subject, brand, date, and text are examples and that this overrides recipe sample defaults (`experimental-editorial-posters/template.md:30`). Several local acceptance lists still spell out sample content (for example desk lamp, fox, `room`, `FIELD 11`, `FIELD 12`). The current text does not provide one common rule for rebinding those sample-specific acceptance statements after a user's subject or wording replaces them. The design implication is left to the parent.
- **Prompt languages have different scopes:** the main delivery contract defaults to a Chinese prompt plus its English counterpart (`oneirloom/SKILL.md:45`), while a recipe says to choose one language per generation run (for example `02-motion-blur/template.md:37-39`). These refer to user delivery versus a single run; they are not inherently contradictory.
- **Local template structures vary:** across the 12 recipes, anchor headings are `Defining anchors`, `Relational anchors`, `Visual anchors`, or `Defining relations`. Check sections range from no separately named acceptance section (`01`), `Checks` (`03`), and `Workflow and checks` (`05`, `11`) to `Acceptance`/`Acceptance checks` (`02`, `04`, `06`-`10`, `12`). The recipe-specific material below remains concrete despite the varied headings.
- **Critique handoff is shared:** main routing, camera, color, design/product, and illustration methods all hand repeated or observed misses to `oneirloom-result-diagnosis`; that method owns attribution boundaries and complete corrections (`oneirloom/SKILL.md:35`; camera `:40-42`; color `:38-40`; design `:27-29`; product `:29-31`; illustration `:30-32`; diagnosis `:16-29`).
