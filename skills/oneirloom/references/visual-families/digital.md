# D — Digital / 数字

**Scope.** Digital-native or computational visual language in a still image.

**Choose this family when** the requested style depends on a digital or synthetic image language, not merely on the tool used.

**Boundary.** A rendered 3D appearance is not an editable 3D asset; follow the requested deliverable and existing method owner.

**Method owner.** [Digital form](../../../oneirloom-digital-form/SKILL.md) resolves geometry, volume, repeated parts, deformation, and connections. Its [form and transformation reference](../../../oneirloom-digital-form/references/form-and-transformation.md) contains the current authored mechanisms. [Color and light](../../../oneirloom-color-light/references/material-response.md) owns surface response; [graphic design](../../../oneirloom-style-design/SKILL.md) owns composed layout.

**Status.** Foundation note with partial coverage. The linked method covers selected 3D form and identity-to-volume decisions; it does not provide an editable model or a complete digital-style catalog.
