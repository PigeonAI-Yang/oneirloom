# Visual families

Use this optional index when a formed scene has an open visual direction or a family-level request needs a route. Send unformed feelings and fragments to dream weaving. Send a settled style to its owner. For a requested medium such as watercolor, use that medium's method directly.

The ten families overlap; they are not mutually exclusive media. Materials and craft describe visual treatment across media. Fashion and product describe a subject or use. These notes cover selected routes, not a complete catalog or validated recipe set.

Recipe tags point to optional routes in neighboring families without changing the recipe owner. [Product surface study](../../../oneirloom-style-photography/templates/product-surface-study/template.md) uses M/P/T tags. [Product-use instant](../../../oneirloom-style-photography/templates/product-use-instant/template.md) uses C/P/T. [Seasonal tabletop display](../../../oneirloom-space-conception/templates/seasonal-product-display/template.md) uses S/T/G. Follow a tag only when the recipe's selection condition fits the request.

**Evidence reading.** Every family note links a method and recipe to case evidence and output checks. Authored guidance, historical inspected cases, current single-example results, and repeat or reproduction evidence are distinct states. One does not imply another. An old example does not supply product identity, facts, wording, or aspect ratio for a new brief.
