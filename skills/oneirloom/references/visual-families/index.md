# Visual families

Use this optional index when a formed scene still has an open visual direction, when the user asks to compare styles across media, or when a family-level request needs a route. Unformed feelings and fragments go to dream weaving. A settled style goes directly to its owner, and a clearly requested medium such as watercolor goes straight to that medium's method. The ten family names are overlapping search dimensions, not mutually exclusive media. Materials and craft describe visual treatment across media; fashion and product describe subject or use. Read only the notes that help compare relevant mechanisms, then use their method owners. This directory contains authored foundation notes with partial coverage; it is not a complete catalog or a set of validated recipes.

The ten family labels and codes organize this collection's visual discovery notes. The local notes record selected routes and boundaries. Their scope and remaining gaps vary by family, and the links do not claim that every category has complete guidance or validated results.
