# A — Illustration / 插画

**Scope.** Image-making through drawn, painted, printed, or collaged marks and shapes.

**Choose this family when** the image is constructed through illustrative marks rather than photographic capture alone.

**Boundary.** Illustration supplies the image-making method; composed text and layout remain with graphic design.

**Method owners.** [Illustration](../../../oneirloom-style-illustration/SKILL.md) owns drawn, painted, printed, and collaged marks. [Graphic design](../../../oneirloom-style-design/SKILL.md) owns composed layouts. Use [craft construction](../../../oneirloom-craft-construction/SKILL.md) when physical assembly is the decision.

**Status.** Foundation note with partial coverage. The route distinguishes illustrative marks from physical construction and graphic layout; it does not catalogue all illustration techniques or validate a recipe.
