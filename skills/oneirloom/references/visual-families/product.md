# T — Product / 产品

**Scope.** Product identity and product presentation as the central image content.

**Choose this family when** an object, its identity, or its presentation is the primary subject.

**Boundary.** Product is a subject dimension, not a medium. Preserve known product facts and keep unknowns explicit; label invented concepts as conceptual rather than factual.

**Method owners.** [Product art direction](../../../oneirloom-product-art-direction/SKILL.md) preserves evidence and facts for real products. [Photography](../../../oneirloom-style-photography/SKILL.md) owns product photography, and [graphic design](../../../oneirloom-style-design/SKILL.md) owns composed product graphics. Use [digital form](../../../oneirloom-digital-form/SKILL.md) for a fictional shape concept, not to invent a sold product's specifications.
