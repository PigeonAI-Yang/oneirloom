# S — Space / 空间

**Scope.** Architecture, interiors, sets, and spatial arrangements as the primary image content.

**Choose this family when** spatial form or the experience of a place is the main visual concern.

**Boundary.** A visual depiction does not imply a CAD model or floor plan. Use the appropriate composition and medium capability for the requested output.

**Method owner.** [Space conception](../../../oneirloom-space-conception/SKILL.md) resolves masses, enclosure, openings, adjacency, circulation, focus, and depth. [Camera composition](../../../oneirloom-camera-composition/SKILL.md) selects the view after layout; [Photography](../../../oneirloom-style-photography/SKILL.md) owns photographic treatment. Use [graphic design](../../../oneirloom-style-design/SKILL.md) only for a composed layout.

**Status.** Foundation note with partial coverage. The linked method describes visual space organization, not a measured floor plan, CAD model, or engineering review.
