# R — Craft / 工艺

**Scope.** Visual treatment centered on making, assembly, and finish.

**Choose this family when** a crafted process or its visible construction is central to the intended look.

**Boundary.** A craft appearance does not establish that a real object was handmade or change supported product facts.

**Method owner.** [Craft construction](../../../oneirloom-craft-construction/SKILL.md) owns units, joins, thickness, edges, and support. Its [template index](../../../oneirloom-craft-construction/templates/index.md) includes the moved fuse-bead recipe. Use [graphic design](../../../oneirloom-style-design/SKILL.md) for a composed layout and [product art direction](../../../oneirloom-product-art-direction/SKILL.md) to preserve real-product facts.

**Status.** Foundation note with partial coverage. Construction checks and the fuse-bead recipe are available; other craft forms remain outside this limited guidance, and visual mockups do not prove buildability.
