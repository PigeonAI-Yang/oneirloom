# R — Craft / 工艺

**Scope.** Visual treatment centered on making, assembly, and finish.

**Choose this family when** a crafted process or its visible construction is central to the intended look.

**Boundary.** A craft appearance does not establish that a real object was handmade or change supported product facts.

**Method owner.** [Craft construction](../../../oneirloom-craft-construction/SKILL.md) owns units, joins, thickness, edges, and support. Its [template index](../../../oneirloom-craft-construction/templates/index.md) includes the moved fuse-bead recipe. Use [graphic design](../../../oneirloom-style-design/SKILL.md) for a composed layout and [product art direction](../../../oneirloom-product-art-direction/SKILL.md) to preserve real-product facts.

**Construction routes.**

- [Layered paper relief](../../../oneirloom-craft-construction/references/construction.md#layered-paper-relief), ID `craft.layered-paper-relief`, tags M/R, describes separate cut layers through their thickness, overlap, support, and contact shadows.
- [Layered-paper backdrop](../../../oneirloom-craft-construction/templates/layered-paper-backdrop/template.md), ID `craft.layered-paper-backdrop`, tags M/R/T, applies that construction to a separate seasonal product setting without changing the product or package.
- The existing [fuse-bead sheet recipe](../../../oneirloom-craft-construction/templates/fuse-bead/template.md) remains the route for individual beads arranged in one grid plane.

**Status.** One corrected item-4 example passed its visible bridge/tab support, layer depth, base contact, full jar, and blank label checks. The backing top is cropped. Physical buildability and repeat reproduction remain unknown. The fuse-bead record is separate evidence and does not validate paper or counted patterns.
