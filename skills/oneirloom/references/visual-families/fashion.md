# F — Fashion / 时装

**Scope.** Clothing, wardrobe, and fashion presentation as image subject or use.

**Choose this family when** garment design or fashion presentation is central to the request.

**Boundary.** Fashion is a subject dimension, not a medium. Retain supported garment facts and choose photography or illustration from the requested image method.

**Method owners.** Read [fashion styling](../../../oneirloom-character-sheet/references/fashion-styling.md) when outfit coordination or continuity needs attention. A single fashion image does not require a wardrobe sheet. Use [visual analysis](../../../oneirloom-visual-analysis/SKILL.md) for observed garment facts, then choose [photography](../../../oneirloom-style-photography/SKILL.md) or [illustration](../../../oneirloom-style-illustration/SKILL.md) for the image method. [Product art direction](../../../oneirloom-product-art-direction/SKILL.md) owns real-garment product facts.

**Status.** Foundation note with partial coverage. Styling and view continuity have authored guidance; fashion design, patternmaking, and garment construction remain outside this scope.
