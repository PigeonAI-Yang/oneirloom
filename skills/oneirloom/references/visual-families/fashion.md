# F — Fashion / 时装

**Scope.** Clothing, wardrobe, and fashion presentation as image subject or use.

**Choose this family when** garment design or fashion presentation is central to the request.

**Boundary.** Fashion is a subject dimension, not a medium. Retain supported garment facts and choose photography or illustration from the requested image method.

**Method owners.** Read [fashion styling](../../../oneirloom-character-sheet/references/fashion-styling.md) when outfit coordination or continuity needs attention. A single fashion image does not require a wardrobe sheet. Use [visual analysis](../../../oneirloom-visual-analysis/SKILL.md) for observed garment facts, then choose [photography](../../../oneirloom-style-photography/SKILL.md) or [illustration](../../../oneirloom-style-illustration/SKILL.md) for the image method. [Product art direction](../../../oneirloom-product-art-direction/SKILL.md) owns real-garment product facts.

**Single-garment route.**

- [Single-garment merchandising](../../../oneirloom-character-sheet/references/fashion-styling.md#single-garment-merchant-display), ID `fashion.single-garment-merchandising`, tags F/P/T, separates supported garment facts from model, pose, crop, accessories, light, and setting.
- [Single-garment product display](../../../oneirloom-character-sheet/templates/single-garment-look/template.md), ID `fashion.single-garment-look`, tags F/P/T, presents one real garment in one view without requiring a character sheet, outfit board, or fixed pose. Its recipe owner is oneirloom-character-sheet.

**Status.** One item-6 run passed the fictional teaching-top checks. The short upper-chest zipper, continuous teal knit to the complete hem, short sleeves, mock neck, headless torso, and warm-gray scene are visible. The run establishes no real-garment facts, patternmaking, construction, sales result, or repeatability.
