# Person defaults and proportions

Read for person prompts, including new text-to-image and every grid panel.

For a requested young beauty portrait without a specified age, use a concrete adult generation target of 25 years old. Default to East Asian when ethnicity is unspecified. Preserve explicit user choices and relevant visible reference traits; a chosen age is not an age proven from a photograph.

For full-body people, show the complete figure with comfortable room around it; “full-body” does not mean the person should fill the frame. Keep enough of the intended setting visible for it to read, unless the user requests a close-up or tight crop or a reference requires that framing.

Keep requested local body volume separate from overall build. Preserve the overall build specified by the user, reference, or established design. Fuller buttocks or thighs do not imply a heavier face, torso, waist, arms, or calves. When overall build is unspecified, infer neither generalized heaviness nor an extreme skinny default.

Ground local size in the requested region's silhouette, projection, and proportions. Keep fullness terms scoped to that region; a thigh target must not be contradicted by requiring all legs to remain thin. Use whole-body terms such as full-figured, voluptuous, or plus-size only when an overall fuller build is intended. Preserve this distinction across prompt languages.
