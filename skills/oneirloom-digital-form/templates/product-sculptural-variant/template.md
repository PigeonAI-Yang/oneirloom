## Selection

Use this recipe when the requested visual concept deforms a named region of an object while retaining selected identity anchors. For a real item, first inspect its current evidence and resolve product facts through [product art direction](../../../oneirloom-product-art-direction/SKILL.md). Apply only appearance changes the current user permits. Keep the known geometry, sold material, actual construction, labels, brand marks, package information, quantity, and other confirmed facts fixed unless the user explicitly permits a concept-only change. Never present an imagined change as a fact about the item for sale. Keep unknown product facts unknown.

Resolve whether the subject is a real item or a fictional concept before writing the prompt. Keep product facts, evidence, and the permitted change in the task context. Put only the resolved subject and visible change in the image prompt.

For a background-only request, keep the current item as shown and change only the requested background through the relevant product-scene method. For an SVG or 3D deliverable, confirm that the actual authoring tools and requested file format are available, then verify the saved native file. A raster concept render is not an editable SVG or 3D model.

## Teaching example

The example is an original fictional, unbranded geometric tabletop container. It has a broad, low cylindrical body, a circular opening, a short raised rim, and a flat stable base. One lower-side lobe grows outward as a continuous deformation of the body. The opening, rim, base, and opposite side retain their original geometry. The example has no real SKU, brand, label, package, manufacturer, specified material, or supported product claim. Those are authored visual choices, not product facts.

The prompt pair below resolves the subject, identity anchors, deformation region and extent, continuous join, surface appearance, view, tabletop, and light for this example. Use these complete prompts only for the fictional container. For a real item, write a separate complete prompt from its current evidence and the user's permitted change.

## Resolved teaching prompts

### Chinese

```text
一张单体雕塑化产品概念图，中央是一只虚构、无品牌的桌面圆形容器。容器整体低矮宽阔，主体是平滑的矮圆柱。顶面开口呈圆形，周围有一圈短而均匀的凸起边沿；底部是平坦、水平的圆形底座。可见右前侧的下三分之一处有一个饱满的圆形凸起，从筒身连续长出，向外鼓起后以柔和曲面接回主体，形成一体的连续轮廓。开口、边沿、底座和可见的相反一侧均保持原有轮廓与比例。整体表面呈均匀的柔和鼠尾草绿哑光色，边缘和凸起上有宽广、轻柔的高光。以略高的三分之四正面视角完整呈现容器，让开口和凸起清晰可见。容器居中放在浅暖灰色桌面上，桌面延伸为简洁的影棚背景。左上方的大面积柔光照亮容器，正面右侧有轻柔补光，凸起表面的明暗过渡清晰。平坦底座下方有贴合桌面的接触阴影，让容器显得稳固；柔和投影落向右后方。物体周围留有适度空白，画面重点呈现完整轮廓、圆形开口和连续的下侧凸起。
```

- One right-front lobe grows continuously from the body's lower third and curves back into it.
- The opening, raised rim, base, and opposite side retain their requested geometry.
- The lobe reads as continuous body volume. It does not look like an attached disc or rim seam.
