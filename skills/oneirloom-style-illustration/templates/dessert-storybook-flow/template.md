# Dessert-to-storybook flow

This template combines photoreal food photography with a hand-drawn miniature storybook landscape. It describes a visual construction, not a historical style or artist attribution.

## Selection

Use when a photoreal plated dessert or drink is meant to continue physically into an illustrated landscape. A side-by-side collage or a clean boundary between photograph and drawing does not fit this preset.

## Visual anchors

- Keep the requested food on a visible physical serving surface in the upper portion, with any requested drink nearby. The reference uses toast under vanilla ice cream, chocolate drizzle, berries, and oats beside an iced latte.
- Choose a plausible edible flow source, such as melting ice cream and chocolate. Show it crossing the plate's actual rim, then continuing without a gap as a descending S-shaped river with small falls. Begin glossy and photoreal; let the same flowing material gradually acquire ink-and-watercolor edges and paper texture.
- Let the illustrated landscape grow naturally around the descending flow instead of separating it into panels. Establish miniature scale through small figures or familiar objects beside enlarged ingredients. The reference uses hand-drawn rocks and plants, oversized berries as landscape forms, oats as stepping stones, and tiny backpack travelers.
- Gradually shift from warm sunlight on the food and drink to a warm paper ground and watercolor marks below. Keep the plate-to-river connection and the gradual transition legible at the selected aspect ratio.
- Add short copy when requested, preserving its exact wording and line breaks. The reference has three hand-lettered lines, "A little / sweetness / goes a long way," with a gently rising curved underline.

## Adjustable slots

Set the aspect ratio, food, drink, edible flow source, landscape details, miniature characters and their actions, exact copy, line breaks, and lettering ornament from the request. The example subjects and text are not implicit requirements. When changing the food, retain a physically plausible flowing ingredient and its visible continuity over the rim. Scale or reposition the plate, river, optional text, and miniature figures to preserve the same readable transition in the chosen frame. Do not turn the transition into separate photo and illustration panels.

## Prompt scaffold

Resolve every slot before delivery and omit optional drink, character, or lettering clauses that do not fit the request. Provide equivalent Chinese and English prompts unless the request asks for one language. Keep the reference image as an observation source; this scaffold is generalized and is not the exact submitted prompt for the stored reference.

### Chinese

```text
一幅{画幅比例与方向}美食与绘本融合图像。{食物、盛放器皿和可选饮品的具体位置、大小与遮挡关系}。让{可自然流动的食材或浇料}从食物上流下，越过器皿的实体边缘，再连续地变成向下延伸的S形溪流和几处小瀑布。流动食材从器皿内的光泽与写实细节，沿同一条路径逐渐转为手绘墨线、水彩和温暖纸纹，摄影与插画连成完整空间。下方{岩石、植物与景观尺度的食材}围绕溪流展开，{可食用的小物件}形成踏脚石，{微型人物及其具体动作、位置与相对大小}。{上方自然光的方向、投影和食物质感}，下方以{细线颜色、透明水彩、纸张肌理与局部配色}呈现绘本景观。{如需文字，在指定留白位置以指定字体呈现准确文案和换行，并描述所需装饰线条}。
```
