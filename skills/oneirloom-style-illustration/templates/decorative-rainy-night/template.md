# Decorative rainy-night print illustration

This descriptive preset has the Chinese working label `青绿雨夜·装饰版画插画`. It is not a formal historical genre or an attribution to a specific artist.

## Selection

Use this reference only when the user requests the template, or when a reference combines an ornate curled-foliage canopy with an environment-led, print-like scene. Rain or teal coloring alone does not trigger it.

## Visual anchors

Keep the environment dominant, with a layered ornamental frame, a luminous central depth path, progressively paler distant silhouettes, and a small narrative focal point. Use fine black outlines, flat limited-color print-like shapes, light paper grain, and coherent linework and color logic.

For a new scene with no conflicting instructions, use these defaults:

- A 4:5 vertical rainy urban lane, framed by near-black tree trunks and building edges with interwoven wires. A bright cyan corridor leads into the center.
- Densely curled foliage forms an irregular canopy arch with small pale mint and cream sky gaps. Crop a few large leaves into the foreground; fade distant silhouettes progressively lighter.
- An adult carrying an umbrella walks to the right near the lower center. From umbrella to feet, the figure is about one-sixth of the frame height.
- A pale curly-coated dog stays beside the pedestrian and partly overlaps their legs.
- Deep green, teal, and cyan dominate. Reserve sparse amber-orange for windows and an old streetlamp, vermilion for the coat, and cream for the dog. Add fine diagonal cyan rain, broken colored wet reflections, and a few tiny colored lights.

If the request specifies another setting, figure, companion, weather, or palette, substitute those details while keeping the environmental scale, focal hierarchy, ornamental framing, and coherent linework and color logic. The umbrella, dog, coat, wires, lamp, and palette above belong to this scene preset; they are not universal illustration defaults. For reference reconstruction, derive setting, weather, figures, props, and palette from visible facts or user intent; do not import absent scene details from this preset.

## Adjustable slots

## Prompt scaffold

### Chinese

```text
一幅{画幅，默认4:5}竖幅装饰版画插画，描绘{地点与纵深路径}的{时间与天气}。让环境占据画面主体，并以近黑色{两侧框景形体}围出通向明亮{中心纵深路径}的视觉通道。密集层叠卷曲的{植物或替代性装饰纹样}构成不规则拱形顶篷，留出零星浅薄荷绿与奶油色的天空间隙；{前景裁切元素与交织线缆，按场景适配}。远处轮廓层层变浅。让{叙事人物或主体及动作方向}位于画面下方中央附近，占画高约{比例，默认六分之一}，清楚地小于环境。用清晰细黑线勾勒平涂、有限色数的版画感形状，并铺以轻微纸面颗粒。采用{主色与少量点缀色}，让线条、色层与明暗关系保持协调；加入{天气细节}、{地面或环境反光}和少量微小彩色灯点。
```

### English

```text
A {aspect ratio, default 4:5} vertical decorative print illustration of {setting and depth path} at {time and weather}. Let the environment occupy most of the frame, with near-black {edge-framing forms} surrounding a luminous {central depth path}. Dense, layered curls of {foliage or an equivalent ornamental motif} form an irregular arch with small pale-mint and cream gaps in the sky; add {cropped foreground forms and interwoven wires, as appropriate to the scene}. Let distant silhouettes grow progressively paler. Place {story figure or subject, action, and direction} near the lower center at about {proportion, default one-sixth of the frame height}, clearly smaller than the environment. Render crisp fine black outlines, flat limited-color print-like shapes, and light paper grain. Use {dominant colors and sparse accents} with coherent linework, color layers, and value relationships; add {weather detail}, {ground or environmental reflections}, and a few tiny colored lights.
```
