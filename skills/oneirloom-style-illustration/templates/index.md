# Illustration templates

Read only a matching template; use the illustration method when none fits. Image paths and generation evidence belong in the selected template.

| Template | Select when | Defining relations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Decorative rainy-night print](decorative-rainy-night/template.md) | Explicitly requested, or a reference combines an ornate curled-foliage canopy with an environment-led print-like scene | Small narrative figure, ornamental framing, luminous depth path, limited-color print shapes; rain or teal alone does not select it |
| [Halftone portrait cutout](halftone-cutout/template.md) | A supplied portrait should become a vintage monochrome newspaper cutout | Preserve identity, pose, and crop; regular circular halftone; texture confined to the subject; transparent background when supported |
| [Zhiguai narrative illustration](zhiguai-narrative/template.md) | Chinese folkloric or strange-tale illustration is requested, especially a human scene with quiet unease or subtle supernatural detail | Aged paper-like ground, hand-drawn ink and wash, restrained color, scene-specific figure and environment scale, layered depth, and a subdued uncanny trace when it fits the story |
| [Dessert-to-storybook flow](dessert-storybook-flow/template.md) | A photoreal plated dessert should flow into a miniature illustrated landscape | Keep the edible spill visibly continuous across the plate rim into an S-shaped ink-and-watercolor river, with a gradual photo-to-paper transition |

For a creative ad based on a supplied product image, follow [product art direction](../../oneirloom-product-art-direction/SKILL.md) to inspect the item before choosing an illustration treatment.
