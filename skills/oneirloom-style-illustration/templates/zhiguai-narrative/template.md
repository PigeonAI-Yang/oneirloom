# Zhiguai narrative illustration

This descriptive preset adapts the visual language of aged Chinese folkloric narrative illustration. It is not a claim about one historical zhiguai art school or a specific artist.

## Selection

Use when a user requests a Chinese zhiguai, strange-tale, or folkloric illustration, particularly when human emotion sits within a quiet, uncanny atmosphere. Select it for the requested narrative treatment, not for a fox, tree, mist, or old-paper texture by itself.

## Visual anchors

- Use an aged paper- or silk-like ground with hand-drawn ink contours, layered wash, visible fibers, and slight irregularity in line and pigment.
- Keep color restrained and locally placed. Let ink, muted mineral tones, and differences in value carry the scene; choose the palette for the requested story.
- Give the scene a deliberate tonal contrast: the focal moment can feel calm, intimate, or mournful while the surroundings hold a quiet sense of strangeness.
- Establish readable foreground, focal area, and distance. State positions and relative scale for the figures and environment from the scene rather than inheriting the sample composition.
- Keep any supernatural trace tied to the story and subordinate to its main action. A faint distant light, an anomalous shadow, an out-of-season bloom, or a partially obscured form are options when they fit.

### Chinese

```text
一幅{按本次需求确定的画幅比例}中国志怪叙事插画，描绘{具体事件、地点与时刻}。{人物或主体的数量、位置、相对大小、姿态、动作、视线、接触与遮挡关系}。{前景形体如何围合或衬托叙事焦点，以及远方环境的位置}，近处细节清晰，远景借{雾气、云烟或其他环境层次}逐层退开。{表情、身体张力与环境异样如何共同呈现本次故事的情绪}。{符合故事的一处细微异象及其具体位置与大小}。以{旧纸或绢本}为底，运用{手工墨线、层叠水墨或矿物色}塑造形体，呈现可见的纸绢纤维、浓淡层次和自然起伏的笔触。采用{克制的主色与局部色点}，{人物细线、环境粗笔、暗部与留白之间的具体关系}。
```
