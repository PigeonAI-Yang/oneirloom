## Overview

Krea 2 is Krea's first foundation image model, trained from scratch to give you full control over the look, feel, and creative direction of every image you generate. It ships in three variants — **Medium**, **Large**, and **Medium Turbo** — and supports the most advanced style-transfer system on the market, moodboards, and Generative Sliders.

Use Krea 2 when you want expressive, aesthetic results that don't look like generic AI output: editorial illustration, photorealism with real texture, character work, fashion, worlds, posters, and any project where style and creative direction matter.

## Variants

| Variant | Best for | Speed | Where to use |
| - | - | - | - |
| **Krea 2 Medium** | Stable, general-purpose output. Illustration, anime, painting, expressive styles. | Standard | [krea.ai/image](https://krea.ai/image) |
| **Krea 2 Large** | Photorealism, raw textures, motion blur, the richest output. | Slower | [krea.ai/image](https://krea.ai/image) |
| **Krea 2 Turbo** | Fast, low-cost iteration on expressive illustration and concepts. | Fastest | See the [Krea 2 Turbo](/docs/user-guide/features/krea-2-turbo) page |

<Tip>
  Start with **Krea 2 Medium** for everyday work. Move up to **Krea 2 Large** when you want the richest texture or full photorealism. Use **Krea 2 Turbo** to explore prompts and visual directions quickly before committing to a polished pass.
</Tip>

## Getting Started

1. **Go to Image Generation** — Navigate to [krea.ai/image](https://krea.ai/image).
2. **Select a Krea 2 variant** — Open the model picker and choose Krea 2 Medium, Krea 2 Large, or Krea 2 Turbo.
3. **Write your prompt** — Describe the subject, setting, mood, and any specific style direction.
4. **Choose an aspect ratio** — Pick from square, portrait, landscape, cinematic, or mobile formats.
5. **Add visual direction** — Drop in style references, attach a moodboard, or apply a Krea style.
6. **Tune with Generative Sliders** — Open the controls panel and adjust Intensity, Complexity, Movement, and Creativity to shape the result before generating.
7. **Generate** — Compare outputs and re-roll with different slider values, references, or prompts.

## Style controls

Krea 2 gives you four complementary ways to direct the visual outcome. All four work on every Krea 2 variant.

### Style references (Srefs)

Upload up to **4 reference images** to extract a style and apply it to your output. Each reference has a strength slider so you can dial in how strongly it shapes the result.

Krea 2 ships with the most powerful style-transfer system on the market — see the [developer guide](/docs/developers/krea-2/style-transfer) for examples and how to call it from the API.

### Moodboards

A **moodboard** is a saved collection of images that share an overall creative direction — palette, texture, style, mood, or composition. Build a moodboard in the Krea webapp at [krea.ai/moodboards](https://krea.ai/moodboards), then reference it in Krea 2 to steer the entire generation toward that visual world.

Moodboards are the most precise way to set a visual direction in Krea 2.

### Krea styles (LoRAs)

Apply compatible trained styles from your Krea style library to lock the output to a specific aesthetic or character.

### Creativity

Pick how literally Krea 2 should follow your prompt:

* **Raw** — No expansion. Renders only what you've explicitly described.
* **Low** — Stays close to the literal prompt, fills only obvious gaps.
* **Medium** (default) — Balanced. Reasonable interpretation without straying.
* **High** — Strong expansion. Takes meaningful creative liberty with style, mood, and aesthetics.

## Generative Sliders

Generative Sliders are three numeric controls — **Intensity**, **Complexity**, and **Movement** — that shape *how* Krea 2 interprets your prompt without rewriting it. They're available in the controls panel for every Krea 2 variant (Medium, Large, and Turbo).

Each slider runs from **−100 to 100**, with **0 as neutral** (no effect).

| Slider | Negative direction | Positive direction |
| - | - | - |
| **Intensity** | Bland, muted images | Intensely stylized images |
| **Complexity** | Minimal, clean compositions | Chaotic, dense compositions |
| **Movement** | Static images | Strong pose and camera movement |

### How to use them

Keep your prompt steady and change one slider at a time so you can attribute each visual shift to a specific control.

| You want... | Try |
| - | - |
| Clean design, icons, editorial illustration | Lower **Complexity** (e.g. −60), keep **Movement** near 0. |
| Cinematic / fashion / character work | Raise **Intensity** (e.g. +60), add a touch of **Movement** (e.g. +30). |
| Expressive worlds and scenes | Push **Intensity** and **Complexity** positive; add **Movement** for kinetic energy. |
| Quick exploration | Turn on **Random mode** to vary all sliders each generation, then commit once you find a direction. |

<Tip>
  Generative Sliders are independent of **Creativity**. Creativity changes how literally Krea 2 reads your prompt; the sliders shape the visual character of the output. They compose well — apply a moodboard for direction, then use the sliders to fine-tune how strongly that direction is expressed.
</Tip>

For the API version of this feature, see the [Generative sliders developer guide](/docs/developers/krea-2/generative-sliders).

## Open source

Krea 2 is also available as an open-source release, so you can self-host, fine-tune, and train LoRAs on the same models that power Krea's hosted image generation. Two checkpoints are published:

* **Krea 2 RAW** — the undistilled base checkpoint. Diverse and highly malleable, designed for fine-tuning, post-training, and LoRA training.
* **Krea 2 Turbo** — an 8-step distilled checkpoint built for fast, high-quality text-to-image inference.

The two are designed to work together: train LoRAs on RAW and apply them on Turbo for fast, polished generation.
