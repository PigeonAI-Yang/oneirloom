# Icon method source notes

These primary sources informed the method on 2026-10-02. The recipes are local design synthesis. No source image was imported, no supplied image was analyzed for these starters, and no generated example or vector production test is recorded.

| Source | Principle used | Scope and evidence limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [IBM UI icon design](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/iconography/ui-icons/design/) | Use shared construction and visual weight, then refine shapes optically. Guides help a family cohere without forcing each shape onto the grid. | IBM's 32-pixel grid and 2-pixel stroke belong to its own system. They are not defaults for every project. The method adopts general consistency principles, not IBM assets or a claim of family compliance. |
| [IBM pictogram usage](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/iconography/pictograms/usage/) | Pictograms communicate at a larger scale and do not replace small functional UI icons. | Choose detail for the actual display context. This distinction does not prescribe another illustration workflow. |
| [IBM app icon design](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/iconography/app-icons/design/) | App icons identify products and services distinctly while maintaining a family relationship. | IBM brand assets have usage restrictions. These templates provide original design guidance and include none of those assets. |
| [Apple app icon guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons) | Official entry point for Apple platform requirements when actual platform delivery is requested. | The page required JavaScript in the research reader. Its substantive content and export settings were not verified in this survey. Do not infer current Apple sizes, layers, masks, or export rules from this link. |

The method's vector-versus-raster delivery rules and the three starter specifications are local production decisions. The sources do not demonstrate that these templates produce successful icons. Verify current official platform requirements when the task depends on them, and inspect the actual deliverable before reporting production success.
