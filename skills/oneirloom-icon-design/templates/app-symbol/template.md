# App symbol starter

## Selection

Use for an app or product identifier whose job is recognition. Choose the outline or solid UI template for small action icons. For a coordinated brand identity, use the brand method to establish the shared identity and this template for the app-symbol component.

## Visual anchors

Focus the symbol on one recognizable product or brand idea. Preserve fixed brand shapes, colors, and any literal lettermark. Keep the central idea legible when reduced, with enough space around defining features to survive the intended crop. Relate the symbol, background, and container deliberately. Do not assume that every app icon uses the same mask, border, or material.

A requested illustrative or dimensional finish can be appropriate. Its lighting and detail must still support the main idea at the intended viewing size. No unseen reference image is associated with this starter.

## Adjustable slots

Resolve the product identity, metaphor, fixed assets, allowed colors, literal characters if requested, target context, display sizes, medium, output format, and container. With an unknown platform, design an explicitly scoped concept and label any mask view as a preview. Verify current official requirements only when actual platform delivery is part of the task.

Choose genuine vector construction for a requested scalable geometric symbol. Choose raster production for a requested illustrative icon when that meets the deliverable. Do not claim an editable vector result from a raster embedded in SVG.

## Prompt scaffold for an illustrative concept

Resolve all relevant slots and return complete Chinese and English versions under the main output contract. Keep platform controls and inspection commentary outside the prompt.

```text
Create an app symbol for [product identity] that communicates [one focused product or brand idea]. The central symbol is [resolved metaphor and visible construction], with [defining shape relationships and recognizable features]. Preserve [fixed supplied identity features] and use [allowed brand colors with their roles]. Render the symbol in [requested illustrative medium or material], with [lighting and finish that support the main shape].

Place it within [chosen concept canvas or verified platform container], using [resolved scale, open space, and relation to the background]. Keep [essential features] readable at [intended viewing sizes]. Use [background treatment] to separate the symbol clearly. Include [literal lettermark only when requested, otherwise omit this sentence]. The visible composition contains [resolved icon content], with a clear main silhouette and [deliberately chosen level of secondary detail].
```

## Production specification for a vector symbol

```text
Construct the app symbol for [product identity] as genuine editable vector geometry. Express [one focused idea] through [resolved shapes and relationships]. Preserve [fixed assets and literal characters]. Use [allowed colors and their roles], [viewBox], and [chosen geometry and optical adjustments]. Keep [defining openings and features] readable at [actual target sizes].

Provide [requested source format] and a rendered preview of the individual symbol. Include [container or mask preview] only as scoped in the brief. If platform-ready exports were requested, follow [currently verified official requirements] and supply the actual required outputs. Distinguish source artwork, concept previews, and completed platform exports in the delivery. Report any unverified export requirement or viewing context.
```

## Critique against the current brief

Inspect recognition at the normal display size before judging material polish at enlargement. Check whether the symbol still expresses the intended product idea after reduction or masking. Preserve meaningful parts rather than clipping them to satisfy a generic container. Check exact lettermark characters and internal openings where present.

Inspect requested backgrounds and actual exported files. A platform-style mockup does not establish a valid platform export. A concept image does not establish a complete brand identity.
