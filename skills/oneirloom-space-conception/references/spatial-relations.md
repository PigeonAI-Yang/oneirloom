# Spatial relations reference

Use this reference to make a place legible before describing its camera view. The method owns spatial organization. It does not replace camera composition, photography, lighting, or a measured architectural plan.

## Check the place

- **Masses and enclosure.** Name the dominant volumes and the boundaries that make the space read as a room, hall, set, or outdoor area.
- **Openings and adjacency.** Locate doors, passages, windows, and gaps. State what lies on each side and what each opening lets a viewer see or reach.
- **Circulation.** Trace a connected visible route between entrances and important areas. Keep the route clear of the masses that define the focal area.
- **Focus and scale.** Set the main area against secondary objects. Use supplied dimensions or known objects as scale references; otherwise state only relative sizes.
- **Depth.** Arrange foreground, middle distance, and background through placement and overlap. Do not rely on camera distortion to create a route or fix conflicting object sizes.
- **Viewpoint.** After the place makes sense, select an observation point with camera composition. Photography may describe how the view is captured, but it does not own the room's layout.

## Mechanism

### Mass, enclosure, and circulation

- **ID:** `space.mass-enclosure-circulation`
- **Tags:** S
- **Owner:** `oneirloom-space-conception`
- **Visible mechanism:** Main volumes define an enclosure. Openings connect adjacent areas. A continuous route passes between those volumes, with a clear focal area and readable depth.
- **Variables:** Mass size and spacing, enclosure height, opening position and width, route direction, focal placement, scale cues, and foreground-to-background order.
- **Failure and correction:** If an opening leads nowhere or the route ends at a solid mass, connect the opening to the next area and clear the path. If relative sizes conflict, use one supplied scale reference and restate the affected relationships.
- **Source and evidence:** Independently authored from the local visual-capability plan. No external wording or imagery was imported. This entry is authored and unrendered; it does not prove architectural or engineering feasibility.

### Merchant display zones

- **ID:** `space.merchant-display-zones`
- **Tags:** S, T, G
- **Owner:** `oneirloom-space-conception`
- **Names and search synonyms:** 商家季节陈列、桌面商品陈列区、seasonal merchant display, tabletop product-display zones
- **Updated:** 2026-10-11
- **Scope:** Organize the visible zones of a tabletop seasonal display. For a real product, preserve only appearance and package facts confirmed in supplied material. Keep a paper setting distinct from the product and its packaging.
- **Visible mechanism:** The tabletop's actual front edge frames a clear approach opening. A display island carries the focal product, while a backwall defines the rear boundary. Visible gaps preserve an open route from the entrance around the island. These zones remain fixed when the camera changes.
- **Variables:** Supplied table and backwall boundaries, opening location, island position, focal-product position and scale, gap width, seasonal props, and observation point. Use stated dimensions when supplied; otherwise express only relative scale.
- **Constraints:** Keep the actual table footprint, existing room, fixed display elements, and focal product unchanged when a request changes only the setting or background. Do not infer dimensions, product claims, packaging details, or a larger room. Props stay outside the route and do not obscure the product.
- **Failure and correction:** If the entrance disappears, move the viewpoint after restoring the open gap and continuous route. If a narrow frame hides the entrance or makes the focal product unreadable, reframe without changing the table or product; retain both the visible opening and the complete recognizable product.
- **Source and evidence:** Independently authored in Oneirloom on 2026-10-11 from the [local visual-capability plan](https://github.com/PigeonAI-Yang/oneirloom/blob/main/docs/visual-capability-expansion-plan.md) and this method's existing spatial guidance. No external wording, product facts, or imagery was imported. This text is unrendered teaching guidance; no generated result or spatial feasibility is established.
