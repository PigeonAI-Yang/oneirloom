## Select and preserve

Use this recipe when the tabletop's front edge, access opening, display island, backwall, or circulation gaps determine the display. The space template owns those zones. For the separate cut-paper backdrop, use the companion [layered-paper backdrop recipe](../../../oneirloom-craft-construction/templates/layered-paper-backdrop/template.md), which owns paper-layer construction details.

The teaching prompt below uses a wholly fictional winter berry gift jar with a blank label. Do not transfer those invented details to a real product. For a supplied product, preserve only its confirmed appearance, package, text, quantity, and dimensions. If the request changes only the setting, keep the existing room, table boundaries, product, package, and camera as provided. Do not infer measurements; describe relative scale when dimensions are absent.

## Spatial mechanism and fixed relationships

- Keep the actual tabletop footprint and its visible edges fixed. The existing rear display wall or backboard remains at the back; do not expand a tabletop request into a new room or shop.
- Leave a clear opening at the visitor-facing front edge. Place the display island behind it, with the focal product visibly on the island.
- Preserve clear gaps around the island and an uninterrupted visible route from the front opening past one side of the island toward the backwall.
- Keep seasonal props outside that route and away from the product silhouette, package, and supplied label details. A paper backdrop remains a separate setting, not a new product package.
- Choose the camera after the zones are resolved. Keep the front opening, route, backwall, and focal product visible. A narrower frame may reduce empty margins, but it must retain the opening and the complete recognizable product without distorting its proportions.

## Adjustable variables

When the supplied scene leaves a choice open, adjust the entrance width or position, the display island's placement, the route's side or width, relative product scale, seasonal accents, and observation point. Keep the supplied tabletop footprint, backwall, real product, and any fixed island in their established positions.

## Prompt scaffold

This complete Chinese and English prompt is an original fictional teaching example. Adapt the scene to supplied confirmed facts before using the recipe for a real product.

### Chinese

```text
为一款虚构的冬季莓果礼品罐创作桌面节令陈列的静态视觉概念。以长方形桌面及其后缘的小型展示背板构成完整、紧凑的陈列空间，桌面、背板和展示岛的边界清楚稳定。商品是一只琥珀色玻璃莓果礼品罐，罐身带有浅奶油色空白标签，作为唯一的视觉焦点。桌面、商品和展示岛按自然协调的相对比例呈现。

桌面前缘中央呈现一个清楚开放的访客入口，入口之后是低矮展示岛，礼品罐稳稳居于岛面中央。展示岛与后缘背板之间有明确间隙；从前缘入口沿展示岛右侧通向后方的路线连续、清楚且保持空旷。少量暗红莓果和暖白冬季剪影作为季节装饰，位于路线两侧，强化焦点商品与空间层次。

礼品罐的玻璃轮廓、罐盖与空白标签完整可见，纸艺冬季剪影作为与商品分离的后景。用轻微的高角度三分之四视角同时呈现前缘入口、空旷路线、完整礼品罐、展示岛和后缘背板。紧凑的构图保留入口和商品的完整辨识度，并自然维持商品比例。采用柔和、中性的商用静物光，清楚呈现商品轮廓、空白标签、空间层次与自然接触阴影。
```

### English

```text
Create a still visual concept for a tabletop seasonal display featuring a fictional winter berry gift jar. A rectangular tabletop and its small rear display backboard form a complete, compact display space with clear, stable boundaries. The sole focal product is one amber-glass berry gift jar with a blank pale-cream label. Keep the tabletop, jar, and display island in natural relative proportion.

Show a clear, open visitor entrance at the center of the tabletop's front edge. Place a low display island behind the entrance, with the jar centered on its surface. Leave a definite gap between the island and rear backboard. Keep a continuous, open route from the front entrance along the island's right side toward the rear. Use a few dark-red berries and warm-white winter silhouettes as seasonal accents beside the route, framing the focal product and spatial layers.

Show the jar's glass silhouette, cap, and blank label in full. Place the winter paper-art silhouettes as a separate setting behind the product. Use a slightly elevated three-quarter view that shows the front entrance, open route, complete jar, display island, and rear backboard together. Keep the composition compact while retaining the visible entrance and the jar's complete recognizable proportions. Use soft neutral commercial still-life lighting to clarify the product silhouette, blank label, spatial layers, and natural contact shadows.
```

## Repairs

If the entrance or route is hidden, restore the open front-edge gap and adjust the viewpoint only after the route reads clearly. If the product becomes hard to read in a narrow frame, show its full recognizable silhouette and supplied package details before tightening margins. If the scene changes the room, tabletop, or real product during a background-only edit, restore those supplied elements and change only the requested setting.

## Output checks

- The visitor-facing front entrance remains visibly open, with a clear gap between the display island and rear board.
- A visible route passes beside the island on the same continuous tabletop surface, without a trench or level break.
- Relative scale reads clearly, and the complete product and its supplied package remain visible.
