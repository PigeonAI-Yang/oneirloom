# Craft templates

Read a template only when its construction relations fit the request. Keep each recipe's palette, display angle, and presentation choices local to that template. A visual recipe does not establish a counted pattern or physical buildability.

| Template | Select when | Defining construction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Physical fuse-bead artwork](fuse-bead/template.md) | Convert a supplied subject into a fuse-bead craft image | Uniform hollow cylindrical beads, one bead per grid cell, source-derived silhouette and colors, a single-bead-thick piece, and a separate near-overhead display view |
| [Layered-paper seasonal backdrop](layered-paper-backdrop/template.md) | Add a separate layered-paper setting behind or beside a seasonal merchant product display | Distinct cut-paper layers, visible contours and thickness, clear depth gaps, visible support relationships, and contact shadows without changing the product or package |
