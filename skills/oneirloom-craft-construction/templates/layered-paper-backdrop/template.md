## Select and preserve

Use this recipe when a seasonal merchant display needs a cut-paper setting distinct from the product. This craft template owns the visible paper construction; the [seasonal tabletop display template](../../../oneirloom-space-conception/templates/seasonal-product-display/template.md) owns the entrance, table bounds, display island, route, and backwall placement.

For a real product, carry forward only details confirmed by supplied material. Preserve the product, package, count, and supplied label as separate and unobscured. The fictional gift jar below is only a teaching subject; do not transfer its details to a real product.

This recipe describes visible support relationships only. It supplies no assembly instructions or safety assessment.

## Visible mechanism and construction anchors

- Use separate matte cut-paper sheets with distinct stepped or curved contours. Show their sequence through overlap, exposed cut edges, and a small visible depth gap.
- Show the raised shapes visibly meeting a backing plane or support points. Keep the support relationship readable without prescribing hidden fasteners, fabrication steps, or load-bearing claims.
- Let the base plane meet the tabletop and show contact shadows beneath the base and where the paper layers overlap. Use light to reveal paper thickness without turning the layers into a flat printed picture.
- Keep the product and package in front of or beside the paper setting, fully readable and unchanged. The paper must not cover, wrap, or become part of the product.

## Adjustable variables

Adjust layer count, cut silhouette, overlap order, depth gap, paper colors, and display angle to fit the visual request. Keep the confirmed product and its package separate from those setting choices.

## Repairs

If the backdrop reads as a print, separate the cut contours into visible layers and restore edge thickness, overlap, and contact shadows. If a layer seems to float, show where it meets the backing or a support point. If paper obscures the product or merges with its package, move only the paper setting and preserve the confirmed product unchanged.

## Output checks

- Distinct cut-paper layers show their separate thickness, overlap order, and visible depth.
- Raised shapes meet a visible bridge, backing plane, or support point. The base contacts the tabletop.
- The complete product, package, and supplied label remain unobscured. A visual pass does not establish physical buildability.
