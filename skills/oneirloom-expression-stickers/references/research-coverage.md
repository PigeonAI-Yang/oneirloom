# Sticker-template research coverage

This map records the evidence checked on 2026-10-02 and its use in the Oneirloom template collection. It accounts for nine industry-design entries, six marketplace or generator entries, and seven direct platform-specification URLs. Supporting and inaccessible leads remain separate.

The collection contains fourteen original method adaptations for DreamWeaver. It does not redistribute third-party art, downloaded templates, paid source files, or a provider's editable style file. A named local template is not a claim that an equivalent downloadable source template exists.

## Visual evidence and portability

The external research read text and listing descriptions. It did not inspect third-party image pixels, run a generator, download a template, import a source file, or submit a sticker pack. Source wording can establish a named inventory or a described workflow; it cannot establish unseen grid geometry, exact pose details, alpha quality, or successful output.

The approved DreamWeaver front image was actually inspected for this adaptation. Its local portable copy establishes the identity reference only. The research snapshot remains dated 2026-10-02 and does not include the separate local production performed on 2026-10-03. See the [template index](../templates/index.md) for the fourteen local examples and the [generated catalog](../assets/generated/catalog.json). Their AI inspection does not test recipient interpretation, platform imports, or animation behavior.

Runtime dependencies are the packaged [mascot reference](../assets/mascot-reference.png), [brand lockup](../assets/brand-lockup.svg), [brand guide](oneirloom-brand.md), [platform profiles](platform-profiles.md), and linked template files. The original URLs preserve attribution and verification paths. No template requires a research-workspace path, downloaded third-party material, paid asset, plugin installation, or source-provider account to use its original local method.
