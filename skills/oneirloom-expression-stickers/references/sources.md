The 2026-10-02 sticker research informed this revision; separate local production evidence was recorded on 2026-10-03. | [Chris Hurtt: Squash and Stretch](https://www.animationmentor.com/blog/squash-and-stretch-the-12-basic-principles-of-animation/), Animation Mentor, 2017-06-05, article text | Deformation expresses material while retaining the character model. The preceding-turn brief, nearby-intent comparison, recipient paraphrase, and permission to choose several items, none, or plain text are local design and review choices informed by these sources. They are not a validated universal test. The head, action, and caption recipe division is also local. A text-led sticker can be valid; hiding its caption is an optional diagnostic, not an acceptance rule.

## Evidence limits

No third-party sticker example pixels were inspected in the research. Text descriptions, teaching articles, inaccessible screenshots, and vendor example code do not substitute for seeing artwork. The approved DreamWeaver front image was inspected for identity. That inspection does not verify any new reaction, and the method and templates make no source-image reconstruction claim.

The three generic recipes retain complete prompt scaffolds without corresponding example images. Separate from the 2026-10-02 research snapshot, the fourteen branded adaptations now include original local reaction examples and authoring demonstrations produced or reused and inspected on 2026-10-03. Their selected files, actual prompts, known settings, and visual limitations are retained in the linked production records and [generated catalog](../assets/generated/catalog.json). Native PNG alpha was inspected, but this does not establish perfect edges, recipient comprehension, encoded animation, or platform behavior. Report semantic use, visual family, file validity, and platform behavior separately; evidence for one cannot establish the others.
