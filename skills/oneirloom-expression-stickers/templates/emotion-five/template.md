# Draw five Oneirloom emotion variants

## Selection

Use this template for a small study of happy, angry, sad, shy, and love. Keep the full front body so that the four arms and dream net can carry the emotion. This five-item scope is a local study, not a platform submission count.

## Source mechanism and local adaptation

[EllyBonBon's Make Stickers and Stamps with Clip Studio Paint!](https://tips.clip-studio.com/en-us/articles/3052) names these five starting emotions. Its process uses sketches, vector ink, separate color layers, simple shading, a merged copy that preserves editable layers, a border, and transparent PNG export. The tutorial also offers face-only or half-body framing. Its images and video were not inspected for this adaptation.

Retain the named emotion set and editable-source method. Replace facial acting with [Oneirloom's approved identity](../../references/oneirloom-brand.md). Use flat illustration and the full body by default. The template does not contain the creator's artwork, source layers, or downloadable pack.

## Design the five variants

Use a local 1024 by 1024 working canvas per emotion. Place the figure and net in the center and reserve clear space above the antennae for an optional caption. Select actual export dimensions and encoding from the [platform profiles](../../references/platform-profiles.md).

In every variant, keep exactly four arms. Each upper arm grips an upper outer blue thread; each lower arm grips a lower side thread. The net always supports one gold crescent. Keep the navy oval and its single gold four-point star visible. Arm positions are viewer-relative.

| ID | Emotion | Body and antennae | Four-arm and net variant | Optional exact caption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E01 | Happy | Lift the body and arc both antennae outward | Raise and spread the upper grips; widen the lower pair into a broad shallow hammock | "开心" |
| E02 | Angry | Brace the body upright; bend the antennae inward with tighter arcs | Pull the upper grips apart and hold the lower grips level, making a taut net beneath the seated crescent | "生气了" |
| E03 | Sad | Lower and compress the body; droop both antennae outward | Lower all four grips and draw the lower pair inward, making a deep hammock close to the body | "难过" |
| E04 | Shy | Tilt the body slightly viewer-left; curve antenna tips toward each other | Bring the upper grips inward near the lower edge of the window; keep the lower grips tucked close, gathering the net beneath the crescent | "害羞" |
| E05 | Love | Lean the body toward the crescent; curve both antennae softly inward | Bring the upper rim close to the body while the lower pair cups the net upward, embracing the supported crescent without covering the star | "爱你" |

If editable source is requested, keep ink, body, window, gold parts, blue threads, and lettering separate where the selected editor supports those layers. Preserve the source before making an export copy. Use vector ink only in an actual vector-capable drawing workflow. A raster prompt does not produce editable vector paths.

## Resolved example prompt

```text
Create one standalone shy Oneirloom sticker on a square canvas. Use a front view of a round periwinkle cocoon with small rounded tufts, two arched antennae ending in gold balls, a central uninterrupted navy oval window with exactly one gold four-point star, and a short curled tail on the character's own left, visible on the viewer's right. Keep the periwinkle surface around the oval smooth and continuous. Tilt the body slightly toward the viewer's left and curve both antenna tips gently toward each other. Give the character exactly four small arms with mitten-like curled ends. Each upper arm grips its own blue outer net thread near the bottom of the navy window. Each lower arm grips its own lower side thread close to the body. Gather the woven blue net into a small hammock beneath one gold crescent moon, with the crescent resting on visible supporting threads. Leave the star exposed and separate all four arm connections and grips. Use flat illustration with body and arms #9994E8, window #25244C, star, antenna balls, and crescent #FFD873, threads #71B9E8, and black #000000 outlines. Set the exact caption "害羞" in rounded black letters with a light outline above the antennae. Put a small "Oneirloom" wordmark below the net without touching it. Use a plain white design-preview background and leave clear margins around every part.
```
