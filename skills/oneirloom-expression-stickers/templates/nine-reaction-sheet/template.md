## Complete example prompt

Reference input: use the approved front mascot (local-only `../../assets/mascot-reference.png`) for identity through the selected entry's supported reference control.

```text
Create one 1024 × 1024 white-background concept preview headed "Oneirloom", with nine full-body mascot reactions in a clear 3 × 3 grid below the header. Each mascot has a rounded periwinkle cocoon with tufts, exactly two arched antennae ending in gold balls, a central uninterrupted navy oval containing exactly one gold four-point star, its own-left curled tail on the viewer's right, and exactly four small attached arms ending in mitten hands. Keep the periwinkle surface around the oval smooth and continuous. In every cell, show all four arm attachments and all four hands visibly gripping blue strands of one hammock below the body. Exactly one gold crescent moon rests in the hammock's lowest supported pocket. Keep every pose in the known front view with only small planar tilts.

Top left, happy: upright tall body, raised antennae, spread outer grips, slightly raised inner grips, and a broad shallow net. Top middle, laughing: bend the whole front silhouette into a sideways arc toward the viewer's left, let the antennae follow, hold the outer grips at unequal heights, and keep the inner grips around a centered low moon pocket. Top right, sad: lower the body, droop both antenna arcs, and use four low close grips with a longer narrow hammock.

Middle left, crying: tilt the body down more strongly, lower the antenna balls beside the upper body, spread the outer grips, and drop the inner grips around a deep moon pocket; place the exact small caption "呜呜" below that cell's hammock. Middle center, angry: compress the body, curve antennae inward, pull outer grips wide and inner grips up, with taut upper strands and a loaded curved pocket. Middle right, surprised: stretch the body upright, raise antenna balls apart, lift outer grips and drop inner grips to leave a deep open gap above the supported moon.

Bottom left, love: incline the body toward the moon, curve antenna balls inward, and gather all four grips around a deep close cradle. Bottom center, shy: huddle and tilt the body toward the viewer's right, lower antenna balls toward the tufts, gather right grips near the body, and leave left grips lower and visible around a small diagonal pocket. Bottom right, thinking: tilt the body toward the moon, curve one antenna inward, and raise the inner viewer-left grip and its strand near the oval's lower edge while the other three grips support the net below.

Use matching flat color illustration and black contours throughout, with #9994E8 periwinkle, #25244C navy, #FFD873 gold, #71B9E8 blue, and #000000 outlines. Keep the star within the oval and the moon supported in every cell. Leave clear gutters and enough margin around all antenna tips, tails, hands, and nets. Keep the backgrounds around the connected figures and their held hammocks clear and open. Limit lettering to the exact crying caption and header specified above.
```
