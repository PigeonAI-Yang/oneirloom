# Design sixteen Oneirloom daily reactions

## Selection

Use this template for an original sixteen-item daily conversation pack. Adjust the selected intents to the user's conversations before production. Sixteen is this template's chosen scope, not a universal platform count.

## Source mechanism and local adaptation

[Tencent ISUX's PUPU 3D Sticker: Daily Life](https://isux.tencent.com/articles/pupu3Dsticker-dailylife.html) documents a sixteen-action series, actions constrained by a round body without a neck or legs, stepped key-pose previews, and later file optimization. The complete source action inventory was not visible in the research. The sixteen rows below are original Oneirloom adaptations, not a reconstruction of that inventory.

Use the [Oneirloom brand reference](../../references/oneirloom-brand.md) and approved front identity. Select flat illustration by default. If animation is requested, resolve the key pose first, then use the parent skill's animation procedure. Do not imply that static poses establish tested motion.

## Author the sixteen actions

Use one 1024 by 1024 working canvas per item. Keep artwork inside a comfortable working margin and choose final dimensions from the [platform profiles](../../references/platform-profiles.md). These canvas dimensions are local choices.

UL, UR, LL, and LR mean the four arms at upper-left, upper-right, lower-left, and lower-right in the viewer's front view. Every arm grips its own blue net thread. Each net supports the existing gold crescent. No arm detaches from the body or releases its thread in these poses. Captions are optional exact image text.

## Resolved example prompt

```text
Create one square standalone Oneirloom reaction sticker with the exact Chinese caption "收到" above the character. Show the approved front identity: a round periwinkle cocoon with small rounded tufts, two arched antennae with gold ball tips, an uninterrupted navy oval window containing exactly one gold four-point star, and a short curled tail on the character's own left, visible on the viewer's right. Keep the periwinkle surface around the oval smooth and continuous. Give it exactly four small connected arms with mitten-like curled ends. Keep its body upright and dip both antenna tips slightly forward. The upper-left and upper-right arms each hold an upper outer blue thread at the same middle height. The lower-left and lower-right arms each hold a lower side thread. Together the four grips steady a small blue woven hammock close to the body, with one gold crescent moon resting securely in its center. Separate the four grips and leave the star visible. Render flat illustration with body and arms #9994E8, window #25244C, star, antenna balls, and moon #FFD873, threads #71B9E8, and black #000000 outlines. Set the caption in bold rounded black lettering with a light outline, leaving a gap above the antennae. Place a small "Oneirloom" wordmark below the net, away from all contacts. Use a plain white design-preview background and keep every part inside the canvas.
```
