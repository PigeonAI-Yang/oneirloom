Neutral is the comparison baseline. Hello raises a grip beside the body; celebrate raises and widens both outer grips. Curious changes attention toward one held strand; work weaving changes the strand crossing itself. Handoff moves the supported net to one side without releasing it. These distinctions keep the seven actions from becoming caption variants of the same pose.
