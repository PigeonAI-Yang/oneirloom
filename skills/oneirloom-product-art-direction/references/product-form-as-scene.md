# Product form as scene

Use this reference when a product's visible shape or a supported visible material helps construct the scene. The product can stay in an ordinary context, become part of the scene's architecture, connect to a scene through a continuous material flow, or remain intact beside a separate miniature world. Choose the mechanism that fits the brief and the source. None is a default mode.

## Choose the scene relationship

| Relationship | Use it when | Make the relationship visible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary product context | The item should appear naturally in a setting without a product-specific fantasy mechanism. | Keep the product at its natural scale and preserve its observed silhouette, package, and materials. Use the setting as context. |
| Product form builds the scene | A visible shape, repeated part, or surface can do structural work in the scene. | Name the exact part and what it becomes, then show its contact with the ground or neighboring scenery. Keep the product recognizable. |
| Visible flow joins the scene | A material already supported by the product or reference can visibly continue into the scene. | Show its source, the physical edge it crosses, and one continuous path into the landscape. Keep its material transition legible. |
| Separate surrounding scene | The brief calls for an imaginative world, while the product should remain untouched or offers no fitting integrated mechanism. | Keep the product intact and show a distinct ground, platform, or visible gap between it and the surrounding miniature scene. |

Use the product's own geometry before adding unrelated decoration. A long piece can become a roof, a block-shaped piece can form steps, or a package surface can carry a drawn street when the observed item supports that choice. A confirmed visible material can cross a real plate or table edge and continue as scenery. These are product-specific constructions, not required layouts.

## Keep product facts fixed

Decide which visible product facts must remain literal before describing the scene mechanism. Preserve the observed silhouette, proportions, package shape and seal, visible materials and colors, count, marks, readable text, and supplied specifications. Keep their source and provenance clear.

Treat scene scale, layout, relative placement, miniature architecture, scenery, ground, contact points, path, and medium transition as creative variables. A scale change or fantasy treatment changes the picture's composition; it does not establish a new product fact. Leave hidden contents, ingredients, internal construction, effects, origin, and experience claims unknown unless supported. Do not reveal an unopened product to invent a flow source.

## Build a legible prompt

State the product first, then the chosen relationship. Describe the product's position and scale, the exact shape or material used by the scene, the point of contact or separation, and the direction of any path. Keep the ground plane, perspective, light, and product hierarchy coherent. For a flow, connect source to edge to destination in one visible route. For a separate world, name the platform or gap. Keep internal uncertainty and evidence commentary outside the image prompt.

## Repair common misses

| Miss | Repair |
| --- | --- |
| The idea reads as generic scenery. | Tie one visible product form or supported material to a specific structural role. If none fits, use an ordinary context or separate surrounding scene. |
| The product loses its identity inside the fantasy. | Restore its recognizable silhouette, package, seal, color, marks, and readable text. Reduce or reposition scenery that hides those facts. |
| A flow seems detached from the item. | Name the visible source, the exact rim or edge it crosses, and the continuous route into the scene. |
| A flow implies unknown contents or ingredients. | Keep the container closed and use a supported exterior form, or switch to a separate scene. Do not depict a cutaway or invented material. |
| Integrated scenery looks like an unrelated collage. | Add a visible contact between the chosen product form and scenery. If separation is the intent, establish a distinct ground or clear gap instead. |
| A transformed object could be mistaken for a changed product. | Preserve the package and product geometry; make the fantasy a scale, composition, or surrounding-scene choice. |

## Switch direction without losing valid choices

If the user changes to an ordinary setting, return the item to natural scale and remove the integrated mechanism. If they choose a product-led world, connect the scene to one observed form or supported material. If they prefer a separate fantasy scene, preserve the product intact and make the boundary clear. Carry forward other confirmed choices such as copy, palette, framing, and medium unless the user changes them. When no supported flow source exists, switch to product geometry or separate scenery rather than inventing one.

## Fictional jar example

This teaching fixture has only these stated product facts: an upright sealed jar with an opaque matte-white cylindrical body, a plain silver screw lid, and a blank white label band. Its contents, brand, category, ingredients, and intended use are unknown. The three prompts below preserve those facts and demonstrate different scene relationships. They are original Oneirloom teaching text, not submitted prompts or generated results.

### Ordinary product context

**中文提示词**

```text
4:5竖幅产品静物图。一只直立的密封圆柱形罐置于浅色台面中央，保持哑光白色不透明罐身、平整银色旋盖和环绕罐身的空白白色标签带，罐身与盖子的比例清楚可见。柔和的窗边日光落在罐身和台面上，周围留出干净空间，呈现自然、朴素的日常产品环境。使用真实的静物摄影质感、柔和接触阴影和简洁背景，产品保持原有形状与封闭状态，背景保留干净的无字留白。
```

**English prompt**

```text
A 4:5 vertical product still life. One upright sealed cylindrical jar sits at the center of a pale countertop. Preserve its opaque matte-white body, flat silver screw lid, and blank white label band around the cylinder, with the proportions of the body and lid clearly visible. Soft window light falls across the jar and counter, leaving clean space around it for a quiet, ordinary product setting. Use realistic still-life photography, soft contact shadows, and a simple background. Keep the jar's shape and sealed state intact against a clean, unlettered background.
```

### Product form builds the scene

**中文提示词**

```text
4:5竖幅微缩纸艺场景。一只直立的密封圆柱形罐成为画面中央的高塔形主体，保留哑光白色不透明罐身、平整银色旋盖和完整可见的空白白色标签带；圆柱外轮廓、罐身与盖子的比例清晰可辨。罐身的圆柱形外壁构成微缩纸艺花园的中心立面，细小纸质小径从罐底接入环形纸阶，沿罐身下部弯行并继续延展到周围地面；小拱门、矮墙和植物围绕罐底展开，标签带保持完整露出。温暖柔光、清楚接触阴影、统一俯视透视，纸艺景观与真实静物质感相接，画面保留干净的无字留白。
```

**English prompt**

```text
A 4:5 vertical miniature paper-craft scene. One upright sealed cylindrical jar is the tall central form, preserving its opaque matte-white body, flat silver screw lid, and fully visible blank white label band; keep the cylindrical outline and the proportions of the body and lid recognizable. The jar's cylindrical outer wall forms the central facade of a miniature paper garden. Fine paper paths meet a ring of paper terraces at the jar's base, curve along the lower exterior, and continue across the surrounding ground. Small archways, low walls, and plants gather around the base while the label band remains fully visible. Use warm soft light, clear contact shadows, and consistent elevated perspective, joining the paper landscape to a realistic still-life image. Keep the open background clean and unlettered.
```

### Separate surrounding scene

**中文提示词**

```text
4:5竖幅产品静物与微缩绘本场景。一只直立的密封圆柱形罐以自然产品尺寸放在浅色圆形底座上，保持哑光白色不透明罐身、平整银色旋盖和空白白色标签带，所有边缘和原有比例清晰可见。底座后方另设一块独立的暖白纸面，纸面上绘有微缩花园、弯曲小径、矮墙和细小植物；底座与纸艺景观之间留出一条清楚可见的浅色桌面间隔，罐子独立置于底座中央，景观完整铺在后方纸面上。柔和暖光、真实接触阴影、静物摄影与细线水彩相结合，罐子保持完整封闭，背景保留干净的无字留白。
```

**English prompt**

```text
A 4:5 vertical product still life with a miniature storybook setting. One upright sealed cylindrical jar remains at natural product scale on a pale round plinth, preserving its opaque matte-white body, flat silver screw lid, and blank white label band, with all edges and original proportions clearly visible. Behind the plinth, place a separate warm-white paper ground carrying a miniature garden, winding path, low walls, and tiny plants. Leave a clearly visible strip of pale tabletop between the plinth and the paper landscape so the jar remains independent of the scenery. Combine soft warm light and realistic contact shadows with fine ink-and-watercolor marks. Keep the jar intact and sealed against a clean, unlettered background.
```
