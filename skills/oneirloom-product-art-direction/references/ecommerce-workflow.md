# Collaborate on static ecommerce visuals

Read this reference only for a vague selling brief, relevant external research, unresolved copy handling, or a handoff to another assistant. Product art direction owns product evidence, unknowns, conflicts, and the boundary between product and fiction. The shared interaction contract owns continuity and prompt formatting. Reuse their unchanged instructions and the existing working notes.

## Develop a vague brief

Inspect available product material before asking questions. Ask only what changes the design, such as where the image will appear, whom it addresses, or whether its purpose is a straightforward product view or an imaginative campaign. Do not require all three answers. Reuse the conversation's settled choices.

If alternatives help, offer two or three different visible mechanisms and explain why each serves this product. A breakfast table can show use context, a studio view can clarify the item, and a miniature scene can make its crust or shape memorable. These are examples, not a fixed menu. Once the user chooses, develop that direction into complete prompts instead of requesting another full brief.

A minor staging preference can use a stated reasonable choice. An unknown filling cannot. An intact-bread scene can proceed while an unsupported cut surface remains excluded. Missing evidence blocks only its dependent depiction.

## Research only a useful question

When relevant tools are available, research can inform audience context, competing visual conventions, category composition, or a named sales placement. Keep external examples separate from the user's product material. Record sources and distinguish observations from design interpretations. Similar items never prove this item's facts.

If search is unavailable, continue from supplied material and state that external research was not performed. Do not invent trend, competitor, or market findings. After an authentication challenge, denial, or rate limit, stop affected online actions. Continue independent local work and identify the source gap. Research is not a prerequisite for a useful prompt.

## Resolve missing copy without changing the promise

Use exact supplied words and keep unknown claims or dates out of the composition. If exact package or brand wording is required but unreadable, request a readable source while developing independent layout choices. The product method's exact-label rule applies: do not silently change the deliverable to an unreadable label. Offer a clearly described alternative for the user to choose. A tool-supported label-preservation path still needs suitable source evidence.

A copy edit preserves the requested literal words. Explain any necessary line break or image shift. Reflow does not authorize a new ingredient or a return to an earlier rejected scene.

## Separate a collaboration brief from an image prompt

For material used in another chat, label the collaboration brief as instructions for the assistant. It may carry product evidence and its sources, unknowns, settled choices, current creative direction, reference roles, and how to continue revisions. Keep the final image prompt separately labeled and complete. It describes the visible target, not research chores, audit questions, skill invocation syntax, or an instruction to interview the user.

For Doubao, ordinary guidance can say which supplied image to upload as product reference and which to use only for style. Put that guidance outside copyable generation prose. Use only controls established for the actual entry. The shared contract's portable-use limits and language rules still apply.

For example, an assistant brief can say that filling is unknown and that the chosen concept uses the observed crust as a station roof. Its separate image prompt describes the intact bread roof, miniature scene, position, contact, and layer relationships. The fictional station remains scenery. Do not export the internal error list as a negative prompt or promise identity locking and label reconstruction.

Use any historical worked exchange only as an illustration. Actual input, reply, source inspection, and result records are required to establish a new run.
