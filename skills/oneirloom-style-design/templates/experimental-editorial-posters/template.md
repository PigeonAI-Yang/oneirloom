# Experimental editorial poster collection

## Selection conditions

Use this collection when one of its twelve image-and-type mechanisms supports the current message and content. Choose one recipe. Combine mechanisms only when the user requests a hybrid. The labels are working descriptions of visible treatments, not claims about the original creator, school, technique, font, or model.

Read the owning method's [graphic-design process](../../SKILL.md). Understand the current message and subject, observe the selected reference image, then design the adaptation before choosing production. This sequence applies to all twelve recipes. Inspect the matching style crop before relying on its recipe text. Each recipe supplies its particular relations, choices, and failure conditions.

## Bind the recipe to the current brief

Carry the selected recipe into the SOP's same internal notes. Start with the subject's identity, connected parts, action, and meaning. Use the observed composition to resolve visible body portions, directions, crop, hierarchy, exact copy, text interleaving, accents, open space, palette, and layer order. Distinguish distinct subjects from versions of one identity and local tonal interruptions from displaced fragments. Explain the material departures from the reference before choosing tools. Do not create a second template-specific workflow or ledger.

Resolve what each fragment join exposes: background, an adjacent or overlapping piece, or lower-layer imagery. Separate interior coverage from exterior silhouette steps. Also resolve the paper or other base, image medium, and final ink or digital finish. These choices depend on the selected source and confirmed intent. Recipe 06's continuous photographic coverage and aged printed sheet do not impose those choices on separated slices such as recipe 07 or on clean digital work.

The following rules override archived sample defaults and any sample wording retained in a local evidence paragraph:

- The current user's fixed choices take precedence. A sample subject, species, pose, brand, title, date, font description, count, or ratio is not a universal requirement.
- Use exact requested wording. Add supporting copy only when it is supplied or explicitly requested as a writing task. Omit unprovided notes, dates, captions, and footers, then rebalance the remaining hierarchy. Never carry over OPUS 5.5 branding, logos, claims, or wording unless requested.
- Resolve open visual choices from the brief and the selected mechanism. A named local fallback, such as recipe 07's seven slabs when count is unspecified, applies only to that recipe. Record the resulting current count and check that count.
- Treat numerical sample sizes as design choices unless a recipe explicitly identifies a visible source estimate. The crops support approximate relationships, not invented exact original coordinates. Archived 2:3 examples do not impose 2:3 on every future user.
- Map source relations onto the understood subject before adding surface style. Cropping, grayscale, and fragmentation may preserve identity, but an emblem alone may lose the subject's action or meaning. Resolve the role of meaningful parts before omitting or reinterpreting them. Preserve actual anatomy instead of fitting it to the source subject's silhouette.
- Preserve explicit full-body, color, or other user constraints. If they conflict with the source mechanism, resolve and label an adaptation. Do not silently drop the user's requirement or call the result a literal reconstruction. Return only a tradeoff that available evidence and the brief cannot settle to the main skill.
- Rebind every local acceptance condition to the resolved subject, exact copy, chosen accent, format, crop, and count. Historical observations continue to name the actual old subject and wording. They do not become future acceptance requirements.

## Reference roles and shared direction

The archived twelve-poster sheet is a style catalog. Use only the selected recipe's single matching crop as the style reference for a run. Keep a supplied content or identity image separate, with its role stated explicitly. Do not use the full sheet as the generation reference.

These recipes often share warm off-white paper, dark type, and editorial scale contrast, but their mechanisms differ. Recipe 02 may remain monochrome, while recipe 11 makes translucency and its chosen image color primary. Resolve accent coverage in the local composition. A common texture or color filter does not transfer a recipe's crop, shape connections, or image/type interaction.

Use a sketch when it helps test the design, with actual copy and enough subject structure to assess identity, direction, and overlap. Its contours remain provisional. A small centered subject or disconnected type may fail a recipe even if all its named elements are present. Resolve those relationships before execution. This internal check requires no extra user approval.

## Production and delivery

Choose direct generation, native design, or compositing after the adaptation is resolved. Base the choice on the requested deliverable, tools, text accuracy, and observed limitations. Honor explicit code, vector, or single-model requests. No recipe requires a universal placeholder-mask or separate-asset pipeline.

Each recipe links complete Chinese and English historical sample prompts. Preserve them as evidence. Their wording and production approach do not settle a new design. Use the selected image and current brief to write a complete prompt or production specification.

For a whole-composition prompt, resolve this scaffold into natural language:

> Create [requested graphic and format] communicating [message]. Show [subject identity and meaningful action] through [visible views, body portions, direction cues, and chosen transformation]. Organize [image mass, crop, text hierarchy and exact wording, interleaving and layer order, secondary copy, accent boundaries, open space, and any local interruption]. At fragment joins, retain [chosen image coverage, overlap, underlying imagery, or intentional gaps]. Use [regional palette and image medium] on [paper or other base], with [ink-edge or digital finish and its relationship across image, type, and accent].

For a composite, carry the same composition decisions into the layout. Supply complete prompts only for assets that need generation, with their identity, view, material, usable framing, and required connections. Keep final placement and type decisions in the layout instructions. Confirm actual alpha or masking support before relying on it, and inspect assets before placement. Revise provisional contours when actual anatomy requires it.

Carry join coverage and final material finish into the assembly instructions as well as any relevant asset prompts. Photographic content can retain convincing material detail while its final reproduction shares a paper and ink appearance with type and accents.

Deliver complete Chinese and English prompts for prompt-only work under the main language contract. Keep entry controls outside copyable prompts and identify untested outcomes. For a requested artifact, deliver one selected poster by default, its final preview, and actual inspection status. Include editable source and assets when the chosen path or request warrants them. Make a contact sheet only when requested.

Example requests include “用织梦师的实验编辑海报07号，主体换成咖啡豆，标题是 BREAK YOUR ROUTINE，做一张单独海报。” and “按10号纸感大字风格处理这句话，保留黄色圈画。”

## Acceptance and evidence boundary

Apply the local checks to the resolved adaptation and actual result. Review the intended meaning and identity, then the whole image-and-text language. Check versions, directions, visible portions, crop, type hierarchy and overlap, accents, local interruptions, and open space together before detail. Inspect exact copy and applicable product facts, materials, and edges at output size. Separate source fidelity from prompt or production compliance. Return failures to understanding, observation, design, or execution as the evidence requires. A precise mask or editable layout does not establish artistic success.

At whole-page scale and intended viewing size, check the chosen coverage or deliberate gaps at fragment joins. Check the intended relationship between paper or other base, image medium, and final finish across type and accents. Preserve readable words and subject detail. Neither blank holes nor an aged texture is a collection-wide default.

The crops document visible source treatments at the supplied raster resolution. They do not establish a generation capability, source production method, exact cut count, or repeatability. Keep observations, interpretations, sample choices, and unknowns distinct.

Historical prompt files, images, and JSON records remain unchanged. Their original inspections and recorded defects apply to those outputs only. In particular, a saved baseline can differ from the actual submitted prompt, as documented locally. This instruction revision has not been submitted as a new image-generation run and does not validate the revised mechanisms. Chinese runs, model identity, and repeatability remain unknown or untested where the local records say so.

The unchanged source sheet and its crop coordinates, dimensions, and hashes are recorded in [reference.json](reference.json). The archived source is [images/reference-sheet.png](images/reference-sheet.png).
