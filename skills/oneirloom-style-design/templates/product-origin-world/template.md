# Historical case: accepted chocolate poster

This folder preserves one user-accepted chocolate advertisement: its exact Chinese prompt in [prompt.zh.txt](prompt.zh.txt), the supplied result image in [images/example-01.webp](images/example-01.webp), and the observation record in [example-01.json](example-01.json). The model, generation entry, and settings are unknown.

The recorded image connects a packaged chocolate bar and its glossy chocolate flow to a miniature cacao setting. In this product pairing, the flow and the sharp tabletop edge visibly relate to the chocolate. This is evidence for that prompt and product only, not a default layout or a repeatable recipe.

For a new product, inspect its actual image and decide on a fitting concept before consulting this case. Do not carry over its river, cacao setting, table edge, or mixed media unless those relations suit the current item and user intent. The original prompt, record, and image remain unchanged.
