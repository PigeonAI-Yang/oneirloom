# Design reference

## Use the relevant domain checks

For posters, covers, infographics, composed ads, and packaging graphics, the [graphic-design process](../SKILL.md) owns the composition and prompt order. Use this table for checks within that work. Inspect the visible structure without forcing it into a listed category. Other rows point to their actual method owners.

For cross-medium style exploration or a family-level request, use the [visual-family index](../../oneirloom/references/visual-families/index.md); this table retains design-domain checks.

| Task | Relevant content | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poster, cover, or editorial layout | Frame, reading order, image mechanism, relative areas, crop, exact copy, type groups, alignment, whitespace, and regional color. | The mechanism and hierarchy survive adaptation; the actual copy fits within safe margins and remains legible at the intended size. |
| Packaging graphics | Brand and copy regions, information hierarchy, display text, and the graphic's relation to the package shape. | Preserve known wording and product identity. Package geometry and construction belong to product art direction. |
| 3D form or render | Use [digital form](../../oneirloom-digital-form/SKILL.md) for geometry, scale, connected structure, and reflection-environment continuity. Use [material response](../../oneirloom-color-light/references/material-response.md) for surface reflectivity and illumination. | Keep the form structurally consistent. A render does not establish an editable 3D asset. |
| White-background catalog image | Use [product art direction](../../oneirloom-product-art-direction/SKILL.md) for supported product shape and facts, and [photography](../../oneirloom-style-photography/SKILL.md) for the photographic view. | Follow the requested crop and preserve the supported product silhouette and color. A complete silhouette is required only when the brief asks for it. |
| Branded product scene | Use [product art direction](../../oneirloom-product-art-direction/SKILL.md) for evidence, facts, and permitted staging; add [photography](../../oneirloom-style-photography/SKILL.md) or this design process for the requested image method. | Keep product facts and focus intact through the permitted treatment. Composed ads use this process for layout. |
| Infographic | Information hierarchy, graphical encoding, supplied values and relationships, labels, and whitespace. | Compare numbers and encoded relationships with supplied facts; inspect label legibility and reading order. |

Exact text, logos, data, and structure require output inspection. When the actual entry cannot preserve critical copy or marks, identify the need for separate typesetting or an appropriate editing workflow. Still deliver the best available prompt when that is the requested artifact.
